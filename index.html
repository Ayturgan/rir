03

Second Dawn prototype

The proof begins inside the Second Dawn cafe.

The current Unreal Engine prototype focuses on believable social presence: NPCs with voice, state, personality, captions, and a setting that already feels like the first room of a larger world. The current scene is anchored by Erik behind the bar and two visitor NPCs: Carla and Nathan.

Erik

Cafe bartender and host: anchors the scene through voice presence, routine, and believable service behavior.

Carla

Cafe visitor NPC: helps prove ambient conversation, social texture, and human presence beyond the bar.

Nathan

Seated visitor NPC: quieter dialogue, reflection, and proof that the scene supports more than quest-style interaction.
